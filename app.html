Retainer Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds up retainer hours from the numbers you type. It has not seen your contract, your time records or your invoices. It does not know what counts as billable, how time is rounded, what your terms say about rollover or overage, or who owes what, and it gives no verdict on any of that. Every number is your own entry.

The pace truth

Hours left in the bank, spread over the workdays that remain. Hours may have one decimal.

The run-out truth

How many workdays the hours last at a steady daily use.

The rollover truth

Unused hours, and the share you enter as rolling into next month.

Retainer Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
