Retainer Math

With 40 hours bought and 13.5 used, 26.5 hours are left, which is 2.21 hours a workday over the next 12 workdays.

Boundary: arithmetic only Retainer Math adds up retainer hours from the numbers you type. It has not seen your contract, your time records or your invoices. It does not know what counts as billable, how time is rounded, what your terms say about rollover or overage, or who owes what, and it gives no verdict on any of that. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 40 hour month with 13.5 hours used, 38 hours left at 3.5 hours a day, and half of 10 unused hours rolling over.

The pace truth

Hours left: 26.5 of 40 bought, with 13.5 used. Spread over 12 workdays, that is 2.21 hours a workday.

The run-out truth

Hours left: 38 at 3.5 hours a workday: that covers 10 full workdays and 3 hours of the next one.

The rollover truth

Unused hours: 10. With 50 percent rolling over, 5 hours carry into next month. Added to the 40 bought for next month, that makes 45 hours.
